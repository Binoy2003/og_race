# BANGLADESH TRUCK RUSH – Authentic Rural Highway Racing

A modern, high-fidelity 3D endless highway racing game built with **Three.js** and WebGL, featuring an authentic **Bangladeshi Heavy Commercial Truck (Bedford/Tata Deshi Truck)** racing through the scenic, peaceful, and iconic **rural countryside of Bangladesh**!

---

## 🚛 Player Vehicle – Authentic Bangladeshi Heavy Truck

- **Hand-Painted Truck Art**:
  - Yellow curved engine bonnet with iconic hand-painted **butterfly grille art (*প্রজাপতি আঁকা গ্রিল*)**.
  - Sky-blue painted driver cabin doors with floral truck art panels.
  - Heavy teal-green front bull-bar / master guard bumper with corner marker posts and genuine Bangladeshi green license plate (**"যশোর-ট ০২-০৯৬৯"**).
  - Ornate carved wooden crown (*taj / mukut*) above the windshield with scalloped borders, minarets, and calligraphy.
  - Large open wooden cargo bed (*dala*) with yellow floral upper rail, green painted truck art panels with **"মা বাবার দোয়া"**, red vertical corner posts, and rear mudflaps.
  - Heavy-duty dual rear truck wheels with painted red steel rims and center lug hubs, plus spare tire mounted beneath the chassis.
- **Realistic Truck Driving Physics**:
  - Heavier acceleration and inertia than a sports car.
  - Weighty suspension body roll/sway when changing lanes.
  - Responsive front wheel steering.
  - Dynamic exhaust diesel smoke and jet boost flames.

---

## 🔊 Heavy Diesel Audio & Authentic Sounds

- **Diesel Engine Sound**: Deep low-frequency cylinder knocking/chugging (~20-55 Hz) and exhaust growl with mechanical transmission gear whine.
- **Authentic Air Brake Sound**: Realistic pneumatic air blast venting hiss (*pssshhhhh-clack*) on braking!
- **Bangladeshi Truck Air Horn**: Dual-tone hydraulic highway truck air horn (*Pooo-Pooo!*) triggered via `H` key or dedicated horn button.
- **Loud, Funny Voice Shouting "TAN DE MAMA!"**: When the player presses `SPACEBAR` or the mobile `BRAKE` button, the game shouts **"TAN DE MAMA!"** with high energy alongside the air brake hiss and comic horn!
- **Rural Ambience**: Morning bird trills (*sparrows/koel*), distant cow moos (*hamba*), and gentle countryside breeze.

---

## 🌾 Authentic Rural Bangladesh Environment & Traffic

- **Cow-Loaded Traffic Trucks (*গরু-ভর্তি দেশি ট্রাক*)**: Encounter realistic Bangladeshi trucks carrying live cows in their wooden cargo beds as traffic obstacles!
- **Green Paddy Fields (*Dhan Khet*)**: Lush green agricultural fields stretching to the horizon with earthen ridges (*ail*).
- **Village Houses (*Tin er Ghor*)**: Corrugated tin roofs (*dheutin*), woven bamboo matting walls (*bera*), and wooden verandas.
- **Roadside Tea Stalls (*Cha er Tong*)**: Traditional wooden tea stall with benches, aluminum tea kettle, and rising smoke.
- **Roadside Grazing Cows**: Deshi cows with zebu humps tied to wooden posts grazing along the roadside grassy verge.
- **Village Ponds with Swimming Ducks**: Ponds with swimming ducks surrounded by banana trees and coconut palms.

## 🎮 Controls

### Desktop Controls
| Action | Key / Input |
|---|---|
| **Steer Left** | `A` or `Left Arrow (←)` |
| **Steer Right** | `D` or `Right Arrow (→)` |
| **Diesel Boost** | `W` or `Up Arrow (↑)` |
| **TAN DE MAMA! (Brake)** | `Spacebar` or `S` or `Down Arrow (↓)` (Shouts voice + Air Brake!) |
| **Truck Air Horn** | `H` |
| **Pause / Resume** | `P` or `Escape` |
| **Quick Restart** | `R` (on Game Over or Pause screen) |

### Mobile Controls
- **On-Screen Touch Buttons**: Left & Right steering pads, dedicated **📢 HORN** button, **BOOST** pedal, and large **TAN DE MAMA!** brake pedal.
- **Swipe Gestures**: Quick horizontal swipe Left or Right across the screen to switch lanes.
